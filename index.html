Model: a full-range constant-product pool, tokens × usd = k, so price = usd / tokens = usd² / k. The fee is taken from the trade input and paid out to the LP position rather than added to pool depth, the way Cetus CLMM fees work. Market cap here is fully diluted (price × total supply). A Cetus position set to a narrow range instead of full range would move less per dollar inside that range and run out of liquidity past its edge. The sim ignores MEV, other pools and the SUI/USD price itself: the $ side is treated as a stable value.
